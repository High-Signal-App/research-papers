# Research Papers

The operator corpus retains 488,491 papers from arXiv, OpenReview, bioRxiv, and
medRxiv for investigating concrete technical and market questions.

## What it is

- An operator-side ClickHouse corpus with 488,491 retained records
- A deployed public analytics snapshot with 478,991 records
- Selected public indexes with citation, reviewer, topic, and graph signals
- Curated reading paths with source notes and export options
- A public cited-answer route protected by Turnstile

The complete semantic index is an operator-side capability. The public browser
search works over selected static exports from the retained corpus.

## Current access

The public site and cited-answer demo currently require no account or checkout.
The repository does not establish a permanent pricing model.

- https://papers.highsignal.app/llms.txt
- https://papers.highsignal.app/api/ai
